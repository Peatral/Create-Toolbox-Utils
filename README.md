</h1>
<p>
Have you ever wanted a silent companion? An inanimate object to spend your time with?

Well, now you can! With this simple addon to the well known Create mod, it is possible to enchant your toolbox with loyalty, making it more loyal to you than the friends that already left you years ago when Create was first released.

Besides automatically returning to your inventory when leaving it behind, it will not be possible for others to access them using the hotkey, making it impossible to confuse your little friends and accidentally stealing the equipment of other players.

This doesn't prevent others from picking up your toolbox and claiming it for their own of course!

Do not fret if JEI doesn't show that loyalty is compatible with toolboxes, believe me, it is.
</p>

<h2 align="center">Loyalty Effects</h2>
<p align="center"><img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/02091f95454d701a1607de6ba9d491ab163a251d.gif" alt="Someone magically picking up a toolbox from a distance" width="400"></p>
<p>
The owner of a toolbox is always determined by who placed it in the world. If someone picks it up and places it again, the owner will change!
</p>
<ul>
    <li>Level 1: Prevents others to see the toolbox when they use the toolbox key</li>
    <li>Level 2: Returns the toolbox to your inventory once you leave the range where you can access it</li>
    <li>Level 3: On returning the toolbox, bound items will be put back into the toolbox - This might be optional</li>
</ul>
